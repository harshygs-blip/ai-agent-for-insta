User Data Deletion Instructions

Compliant with Meta Platform Terms & GDPR

According to Meta Platform rules, apps integrated with Facebook or Instagram must provide a way for users to request the deletion of their personal data.

If you have interacted with The Harsh Seller (ff_trusted_deals1) automated messenger or customer service and wish to have your data permanently deleted from our databases, please follow the simple steps below:

Option 1: In-App Meta Data Deletion Request

  1. Go to your Facebook or Instagram Account's Settings & Privacy.
  2. Click on Settings → Apps and Websites.
  3. Find The Harsh Seller / FF Trusted Deals in the list of active apps.
  4. Click Remove.
  5. Click on the View Removed Apps and Websites tab, select our app, and click Send Request to submit an automated data deletion request.

Option 2: Direct 1-Click Request via WhatsApp or Email

You can directly request immediate deletion of your conversation logs, user ID, and transaction records:

• Email Request: Send an email with subject Data Deletion Request to harshpandeyofficial73@gmail.com
• Processing SLA: All data deletion requests are permanently executed within 24 to 48 hours, and a confirmation confirmation receipt is returned.

What Happens When Data is Deleted?

Upon receipt of your request, our systems immediately purge:

  • All Instagram Scoped User IDs (IGSID) associated with your profile.
  • All message history stored in temporary cache and conversation archives.
  • All payment verification logs older than statutory accounting requirements.
